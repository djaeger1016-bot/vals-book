# About the Book & Author

## The Story

**Doodles and the Backyard Birds** is an enchanting children's book that celebrates the power of friendship, courage, and standing together.

The story follows **Sheldon**, a timid bird who is often bullied by others, feeling isolated and alone. But Sheldon possesses a special gift—unique feet that allow him to navigate the world in ways others cannot.

When a menacing crow and crafty grackle threaten the safety of the bird community, it is Sheldon who rises to the occasion. With the support of his loyal canine companions, **Gracie and Rosie** (two golden doodle dogs), Sheldon learns to embrace his differences and stand tall against adversity.

Together with friends like **Mayor Robbins, Sandi Robbins, Gypsy, and Sara**, they devise clever strategies to outsmart their foes, showcasing the importance of teamwork and resilience. As Sheldon gains confidence, he inspires his fellow feathered friends to believe in themselves and their abilities.

### Key Themes

- **Friendship** - The bonds that form when we support one another
- **Bravery** - Standing up for what's right, even when afraid
- **Standing Together** - Teamwork and unity overcome challenges
- **Embracing Differences** - Your unique qualities make you special

## About Val C.

![Val C. - Author](/images/author-photo.jpg)

Val C. is a children's book author, illustrator, performer, and creative spirit passionate about helping young readers understand animals and learn valuable life lessons.

### The Mission

> "I have written and illustrated this children's book in hopes it will help children understand animals better while instilling teachable moments with helping others—especially when they can't help themselves."

**Val's dream is to open the largest animal refuge on the East Coast!** Every purchase of this book directly supports this mission to save and protect animals in the future.

> "This book comes from the heart and can hopefully save a lot of animals in the future. I can't do it without everyone's help. Please enjoy!"
