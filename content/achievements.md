# Creative Achievements

Val C. has been creating, performing, and inspiring audiences for decades. Here's a look at her creative journey:

---

## 🎬 **Commercial Acting**

**"Partners for Youth" Commercial** - Dade County, FL  
At just 12 years old, Val appeared in this commercial, marking the beginning of her journey as a performer and creative professional.

---

## 🎵 **Music & Performance**

**Band Performances**  
Starting in high school, Val has been a dedicated musician, performing in various bands throughout her creative career.

**Songwriting**  
Val has written many original songs, blending her passion for music with storytelling and emotion.

**Convention Hall Performance** - Asbury Park  
Val performed at the prestigious Convention Hall in Asbury Park, showcasing her talents to a broader audience.

---

## 🎭 **Theater & Playwriting**

**One-Act Play: "Body & Soul"**  
Val wrote and performed in this original one-act play, which was performed at **Bergen Community College (BCC), New Jersey**. The production received a positive review in the local newspaper, highlighting Val's skills as both a playwright and performer.

---

## 🎬 **Screenwriting**

**Screenplay: "Fallen Angels"**  
Val expanded her creative horizons by writing a screenplay, demonstrating her versatility as a storyteller across multiple media formats.

---

## 📚 **Children's Literature**

**"Doodles and the Backyard Birds"**  
Val's most recent achievement—writing and illustrating this beloved children's book that teaches valuable lessons about friendship, bravery, and standing together. The book is now available and bringing her dream of opening an animal refuge closer to reality.

---

## 🌟 **A Creative Legacy**

From commercial acting at age 12 to performing on stage, writing music and plays, creating screenplays, and now publishing children's books, Val's creative journey demonstrates a lifelong commitment to storytelling, performance, and inspiring others.

Every project reflects Val's passion for connecting with audiences and using art to make a difference in the world.
